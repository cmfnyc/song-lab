Bass & Counter-Melody Builder

Bass: four levels

Go up one level at a time. Stop when it sounds good. Simple often wins.

Level 1 — Roots

Play the chord's letter name on beat 1. (C chord → play C.)

Level 2 — Lock with the kick

Play the root, but copy the kick drum's rhythm. When bass and kick hit together, the groove feels tight.

Level 3 — Root + fifth or octave

Bounce between the root and the note five steps up (C → G), or the same note higher (low C → high C).

Level 4 — Walk to the next chord

On the last beat of the bar, step toward the next chord's root. Example, C going to G: C … E – F – G.

Example (key of C, progression C – G – Am – F)

  • Level 1: C | G | A | F — one long note per bar
  • Level 2: C . . . . . . . C . C . . . . . (matches a Basic Rock kick)
  • Level 3: C – G – C – G | G – D – G – D | A – E – A – E | F – C – F – C

Bass tips

  • Simpler under singing. Busy bass is fine in the intro and between lines.
  • Low-note mud: if chords and bass are both low, move the chords up an octave.
  • Pedal tone (Level 2 trick): hold the home note in the bass while chords change on top. Creates tension.

Counter-melody: two rules

A counter-melody is a second melody that fills the gaps in the main one.

  1. When the lead moves, the counter-melody holds or rests.
  2. When the lead holds or rests, the counter-melody moves.

Put it higher or lower than the lead and use a different sound (bells, pluck synth, guitar, strings).

A short counter-melody that repeats is a riff. Riffs make great intros.

Where could my counter-melody go?

  • Intro riff
  • In the gaps between chorus lines
  • After each verse line
  • Answering the vocal hook (call and response)
  • Bridge or instrumental section

In おんぷノート

The app keeps one score at a time, so each part gets its own score and its own PDF.

  1. Save your current score as a PDF first.
  2. Start a new score with a bass clef. Pick a bass-like playback sound.
  3. Check each bass note against your chord chart. The note on beat 1 should usually be the chord's root.
  4. Export the PDF (S2-[title]-bass-v1.pdf).
  5. For the counter-melody: new score, treble clef, bright sound, export the PDF.
  6. Hear everything together by playing each part into GarageBand, reading from your PDFs.
