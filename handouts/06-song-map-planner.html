Song Map Planner

How long will my song be?

Seconds = bars × 4 × 60 ÷ BPM

Example: 40 bars at 100 BPM → 40 × 4 × 60 ÷ 100 = 96 seconds (1:36).

Energy curve

Draw a line across this page from left to right. Up = bigger, down = smaller.

A typical pop curve:

  • Intro: low
  • Verse 1: medium
  • Chorus: high
  • Verse 2: medium, a bit higher than verse 1
  • Chorus: high
  • Bridge: drop down or build up
  • Final chorus: highest
  • Outro: low

Ways to raise energy

  • Add an instrument
  • Drums get busier (more hi-hat, more kick)
  • Melody goes higher
  • Add a harmony or double the vocal
  • A fill or riser right before the section

Ways to lower energy

  • Mute instruments (the mute button is your best tool)
  • Simpler drums, or no drums
  • Lower melody, fewer words
  • Chords held longer
