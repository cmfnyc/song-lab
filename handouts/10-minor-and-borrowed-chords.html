Minor Keys & Borrowed Chords

Every major key has a minor twin

They use the same notes. Only the "home" chord changes.

  • C major ↔ A minor
  • G major ↔ E minor

A song feels minor when the minor chord is home — where phrases start and rest.

Same chords, different home:

  • C – G – Am – F → home is C → sounds major
  • Am – F – C – G → home is Am → sounds minor

Minor loops

In minor, the numbers change to match the new home. Try each and write a mood word.

  1. i – VI – III – VII — Am F C G / Em C G D — Mood: __________
  2. i – VII – VI – VII — Am G F G / Em D C D — Mood: __________
  3. i – iv – VI – V — Am Dm F E / Em Am C B — Mood: __________

(In loop 3, the V is major — E instead of Em, B instead of Bm. It pulls hard back to home.)

Minor doesn't just mean sad. It can be mysterious, angry, determined, epic, or hype.

Borrowed chords

A song in major can borrow one chord from the minor key for a flash of color.

Chord In C In G Feeling Try this
iv (minor four) Fm Cm Bittersweet, nostalgic C – F – Fm – C
♭VII (flat seven) B♭ F Big, rock, triumphant C – B♭ – F – C
♭VI (flat six) A♭ E♭ Cinematic, heroic C – A♭ – B♭ – C

The last one (♭VI – ♭VII – I) is the Mario cadence — it ends the Super Mario Bros. level-complete fanfare.

Rule of thumb: one borrowed chord is color. Three starts to sound like a different key.

Playing them

Letter names (for the chord chart and GarageBand keyboard)

  • Fm = F A♭ C
  • B♭ = B♭ D F
  • A♭ = A♭ C E♭
  • E (major) = E G♯ B

Ukulele (G C E A strings, top to bottom)

  • Fm = 1013
  • B♭ = 3211
  • A♭ = 1343
  • E = 1402, or the easier E7 = 1202
  • Key of G versions: Cm = 0333 · F = 2010 · E♭ = 0331 · B7 = 2322
  • Minor loops in A minor: Am 2000 · F 2010 · C 0003 · G 0232 · Dm 2210

Color chords (bonus)

Add a seventh to any chord for a smoother, "lo-fi" or R&B sound (ukulele shapes in brackets):

  • Cmaj7 = C E G B [0002]
  • Fmaj7 = F A C E [2410]
  • Am7 = A C E G [0000]
  • Dm7 = D F A C [2213]
