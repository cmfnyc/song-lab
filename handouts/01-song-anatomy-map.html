Song Anatomy Map

Every section has a job

  • Intro — Sets the mood. Says "a song is starting." Often just chords or a riff.
  • Verse — Tells the story. Same melody every time, new words every time.
  • Pre-chorus (optional) — Builds up. Makes you feel "something big is coming."
  • Chorus — The main idea and usually the title. Same melody and same words every time. The biggest part.
  • Post-chorus (optional) — A short, catchy add-on after the chorus. Often just "oh-oh" or one word.
  • Bridge — Something different, usually once, near the end. New chords, new angle.
  • Outro — The ending. Fades out, or stops on a big hit.

The hook is the part that gets stuck in your head. Usually a short phrase in the chorus. Sometimes it's an instrument riff.

The most common pop shape

Verse → Chorus → Verse → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus

With extras: Intro → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Verse → Pre-chorus → Chorus → Bridge → Chorus → Outro

What changes when the chorus arrives?

Listen and check every one you hear:

  • It gets louder
  • The melody goes higher
  • More instruments come in
  • The drums change
  • The title is sung
