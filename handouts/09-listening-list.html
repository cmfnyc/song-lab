Listening List

Songs to study, grouped by the idea they show well. You don't need all of them. Pick one or two per session.

Always better: use songs he already loves. Any song with a clear verse and chorus works for mapping — J-pop, anime openings, game music with vocals, K-pop. This list is a backup.

Producer note: preview for content before playing. Use clean/radio edits where they exist.

Song structure and hooks (Sessions 1, 3)

  • "Happy" — Pharrell Williams
  • "Shake It Off" — Taylor Swift
  • "Firework" — Katy Perry
  • "Counting Stars" — OneRepublic

The four-chord loop (Session 1)

  • "Let It Be" — The Beatles (I–V–vi–IV)
  • "Someone Like You" — Adele (I–V–vi–IV)
  • "Don't Stop Believin'" — Journey (I–V–vi–IV in the verse)
  • "Despacito" — Luis Fonsi ft. Daddy Yankee (vi–IV–I–V)
  • "Stand By Me" — Ben E. King (I–vi–IV–V)
  • "Viva la Vida" — Coldplay (IV–V–I–vi)

Grooves (Session 2)

  • Basic rock beat: "Shake It Off" — Taylor Swift
  • Four on the floor: "Levitating" — Dua Lipa
  • Dembow: "Despacito" — Luis Fonsi ft. Daddy Yankee

Pre-chorus (Sessions 5, 9)

  • "Call Me Maybe" — Carly Rae Jepsen
  • "Firework" — Katy Perry

Syncopation and flow (Session 9)

  • "Uptown Funk" — Mark Ronson ft. Bruno Mars
  • "Shape of You" — Ed Sheeran

Minor mood (Session 7)

  • "Rolling in the Deep" — Adele
  • "Believer" — Imagine Dragons
  • "Zombie" — The Cranberries (same four chords as vi–IV–I–V, but it feels minor — a great "which chord is home?" debate)

Borrowed chords (Session 7)

  • "In My Life" — The Beatles (listen for the minor iv chord near the end of the verse)
  • "Hey Jude" — The Beatles (the long "na na na" ending uses ♭VII: I–♭VII–IV–I)
  • The Super Mario Bros. level-complete fanfare (ends ♭VI–♭VII–I: the "Mario cadence")

Basslines and riffs (Session 8)

  • "Another One Bites the Dust" — Queen
  • "Billie Jean" — Michael Jackson
  • "Seven Nation Army" — The White Stripes (a riff that sounds like a bassline)

Bridges and key changes (Session 10)

  • "Love Story" — Taylor Swift (bridge, then a key change up for the final chorus)
  • "Livin' on a Prayer" — Bon Jovi (key change in the final chorus)
  • "Man in the Mirror" — Michael Jackson (key change)

Production (Session 11)

  • "Bad Guy" — Billie Eilish (drop-outs, ear candy, unusual sounds)
  • "Blinding Lights" — The Weeknd (synth layers, energy curve)
